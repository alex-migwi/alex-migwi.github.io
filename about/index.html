About Me

I’m a Developer Experience Engineer and Team Lead, Builder Ecosystem & Product Activation at Builders Space. Based in Nairobi, Kenya and working globally, I focus on making complex systems easier to understand, integrate, and use—by both developers and AI agents.

My work sits at the intersection of:

  1. Developer tooling (CLI, automation)
  2. Documentation as structured, reusable context
  3. Reproducible developer workflows
I’m particularly interested in how AI is changing how software is built—where tools are no longer used only by humans, but also by agents that read docs, write code, and perform integrations.
